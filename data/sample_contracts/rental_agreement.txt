RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made between Mr. Anil Kapoor ("Landlord") and Ms. Sneha Rao ("Tenant") for a residential flat at Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.

1. PREMISES
The Landlord lets the flat described in the Schedule to the Tenant for residential use only.

2. RENT
The Tenant shall pay rent of Rs. 25,000 per month on or before the 5th day of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of Rs. 75,000, to be returned at the end of the tenancy after deducting any dues.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL
This Agreement is for eleven months and may be renewed by mutual written consent.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one month's written notice.

6. LOCK-IN AND PENALTY
If the Tenant vacates within the first six months, the Tenant shall forfeit the entire security deposit as penalty.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Ghaziabad shall have jurisdiction.
