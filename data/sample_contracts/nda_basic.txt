NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is made between Orbit Labs Private Limited ("Disclosing Party") and Vertex Consulting LLP ("Recipient").

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential Information means all non-public business, technical and financial information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including trade secrets and proprietary information.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECIPIENT
The Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party and shall use it only for evaluating the proposed business relationship.

3. NON-COMPETE
The Recipient shall not, for two (2) years after termination of this Agreement, carry on any business that competes with the Disclosing Party.

4. TERM
This Agreement remains in force for three years from the date of signing. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales and the courts of London shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
