EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on this 1st day of January, 2024, by and between:
Apex Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office in Bangalore, Karnataka ("Employer"); and
Rohan Sharma, residing at Indiranagar, Bangalore, Karnataka ("Employee").

WHEREAS Employer desires to employ Employee, and Employee desires to accept such employment upon the terms and conditions herein set forth:

Clause 1. Term of Employment
The employment of Employee shall commence on January 15, 2024 and shall continue until terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

Clause 2. Remuneration and Benefits
Employer shall pay Employee a monthly base salary of INR 1,50,000, subject to statutory deductions, payable in accordance with the standard payroll schedule of the company.

Clause 3. Non-Competition and Restraint of Trade
Employee covenants and agrees that during the term of employment and for a period of two (2) years following the termination of employment for any reason whatsoever, Employee shall not directly or indirectly engage in, establish, or work for any business, firm, or competitor within the territory of India that competes with the business of Employer.

Clause 4. Non-Solicitation of Clients and Employees
Employee agrees that for a period of one (1) year following termination of employment, Employee shall not solicit or induce any employee, contractor, customer, or client of Employer to leave or discontinue their business relationship with Employer.

Clause 5. Indemnification
Employee shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Employer, its directors, officers, and affiliates from and against any and all claims, liabilities, losses, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorney fees) arising out of or resulting from Employee's performance of duties or any breach of this Agreement. Employer shall have no obligation to indemnify Employee.

Clause 6. Limitation of Liability
In no event shall Employer be liable to Employee for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Employee's liability under this Agreement shall be uncapped and unlimited in amount.

Clause 7. Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure
Employee agrees that all trade secrets, proprietary software, and business strategies disclosed by Employer shall remain strictly confidential perpetually, without any limitation in time.

Clause 8. Dispute Resolution and Arbitration
Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be settled by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be New Delhi, India.

Clause 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The courts located at New Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction over all matters arising hereunder.
